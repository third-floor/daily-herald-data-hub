Date analysis

Catalogue dates recognise both YYYY-MM-DD and ISO-style values such as 1954-03-09T00:00:00. Structured derived dates use date_info.date_standardised where present, with a transcription fallback when absent. Relationship labels are preserved rather than assuming every date is a photograph-creation date.

Photographers and maker-name standardisation

The maker field is decomposed before matching. Organisations such as picture agencies, publishers and newspapers are separated from personal photographers. Personal names are then standardised for accents, case, punctuation, surname-first/full-name forms, conservative missing-prefix forms such as LINDINE → Malindine, and a small set of explicit OCR aliases. Ambiguous or genuinely unknown names remain unresolved rather than being forced onto a reference name.

People entity standardisation

People entities are standardised conservatively. The dashboard merges case/accent/punctuation differences, reverses surname-first forms such as Churchill, Winston, and can combine initials such as W. Churchill with Winston Churchill only where the surname/initial combination is unambiguous in the loaded corpus. A surname-only form is merged only when it maps uniquely to one fuller personal name. Original variants are always retained and displayed.
